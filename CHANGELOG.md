# Changelog — Piano Hero

Free webapp (no paid version, no gate). Versions follow the Cedwyn version standard (docs/VERSION-STANDARD.md in unclehowell/cedwyn).

| Version | Date | What changed | Size | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3.1 | 2026-11-11 | An (i) button along the top of the keyboard for every key (88; white keys on the lower tier, black keys on the upper one, each centred on its key). Tapping or clicking it opens a small panel with the party's name as the keys show it, its active days, the first and last year it appears on the grid, and the catalogue IDs of its notes (no links). The panel closes with Esc, its × button or a tap outside it. The buttons are focusable, labelled for screen readers and move with the arrow keys; Space and Enter open them without starting playback. Playback, the filter and the N-of-59 nav are unchanged. | 88 keys, 393 year rows + 152 quarters + 11 months | free |
| 0.3.0 | 2026-11-11 | Keys run from the least active party on the far left to the most active on the far right, so the left half holds only the quietest parties (the busiest of them nearest the centre). The record starts with a four-year count-in (1496-1499) before the 1500s row (the 1,000-year lease, catalogue EV-0002), then one row a year from 1600, quarters from 1988 and months from 2026; records dated before 1500 are left out, and Bute papers dated before 1824 are not placed on the timeline. Notes sound when they reach the centre line of the screen (the hit line moved there; the year counter sits beside it). Steady beat: a gap between two note rows takes at most two beats (0.6 s each), easing through the empty years; the count-in ticks one beat a year. Each note's label shows the record's picture from its Cedwyn page, or a placeholder tile with its catalogue ID, with the party, the catalogue ID, the record type and the period (up to three events per row). Filter: All / Press coverage, later citation & interpretation / Verbatim source record or transcript (classified from the catalogue record's label and type; emails received, WhatDoTheyKnow responses and website captures count as verbatim; chronology entries as later interpretation; the rest show under All only). | 88 keys, 393 year rows + 152 quarters + 11 months | free |
| 0.2.0 | 2026-11-11 | Keys reordered by activity, centre out: the most active party on the middle key (an 88-key piano has two, E4 and F4), the next ones alternating either side, the least active at the two ends; numbers run 1 to 88 left to right. The Williams family added as a party (days on which a received reply, a dated catalogue record or a chronology event names the family). Rows run quarterly to 2025-Q4, then monthly from 2026-01 to the current month, with the switch marked on the grid. Activity counts emails RECEIVED from institutions only: emails sent to them, "Sent" journal entries, ERP request-opened dates and correspondence-thread summaries (dated at the opening request) are no longer counted. Added: WhatDoTheyKnow responses on the case requests (tools/inputs/wdtk-responses.json) and watchtower website changes (cedwyn-watchtower changes.json and watch-events.json). A key shows its active days when tapped. | 88 keys, 152 quarters + 11 months | free |
| 0.1.0 | 2026-11-11 | First release, slot 57 of 59. One key per institution on a real 88-key layout (white and black keys in their true places), each numbered; a row per quarter from 1988-Q1 to 2026-Q4; a note wherever that body was active (central catalogue, ERP request data, forensic activity journal; one count per active day). Pressing a key plays its pitch (WebAudio, no samples) and shows the body's name for 1.5 s. Play rolls the table down onto the keys with a parallax staff behind it; each row plays as it reaches the line; ×2 doubles the speed; long quiet stretches pass faster. Landscape first (turn-your-phone hint in portrait); portrait shrinks to the screen width. No links in the app body. | 88 keys, 156 quarters | free |
