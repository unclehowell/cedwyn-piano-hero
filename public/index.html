Piano Hero · version 0.8.0

0.8.0 · 15 November 2026

Playback pauses at the first event in each year. The left-hand review panel shows one dated record at a time; Next advances through that year in chronological order. Five seconds after the final event is displayed, playback resumes automatically.

The 88 institution keys remain in their fixed order and width. During a review stop, the selected institution key highlights and the bottom strip shows only the institution name. The left panel shows the record date, description, type, catalogue reference and local or record-linked image.

From 2026 onward, only actual disclosure material is shown: released documents, FOI/EIR/SAR responses, search results and records supplied. Acknowledgements, requests, chasers, clarifications and other pursuit activity are excluded.

The 25 July 2025 online-publication milestone for the GHF-E113 YouTube viewing copy is retained as a distinct event; the uploader is unestablished and the item is not conflated with National Library of Wales catalogue item 507738.
